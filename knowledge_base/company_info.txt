Escalation Policy:
If a customer's issue cannot be resolved through standard support, agents may escalate the conversation to a human support specialist. Escalated tickets are typically responded to within 24 hours via email or phone.

Warranty:
All products sold on Customer Support come with the manufacturer's standard warranty, ranging from 6 months to 2 years depending on the product category. Extended warranty plans are available for purchase at checkout on select items.
