FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - Customer Support

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Log in to your Customer Support account, go to "My Orders," and click on the relevant order to see live tracking status. A tracking link is also sent via email and SMS once the order ships.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Address changes are possible only before the order is shipped. Contact customer support immediately with your order ID to request a change.

Q: Do you offer installment/EMI payment options?
A: Yes, EMI options are available on orders above Rs. 3,000 through select partner banks at checkout.

Q: Are the products sold on Customer Support genuine and covered by warranty?
A: Yes, all products are 100% genuine and come with the manufacturer's standard warranty. Warranty cards or e-warranty details are included with the shipment.

Q: What should I do if I received the wrong product?
A: Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and photos of the received item. Customer Support will arrange a free pickup and send the correct product or issue a full refund.

Q: How can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled for free anytime before they are shipped, via the "My Orders" section or by contacting support. Once shipped, cancellation is not possible, but you may return the item after delivery.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping or gift cards?
A: Gift wrapping is available for a small additional fee at checkout. Customer Support gift cards are available in denominations of Rs. 500, Rs. 1000, and Rs. 2000.

Q: How do I contact a human support agent?
A: You can request escalation at any point during a chat with our support bot, or email support@customersupport.com, or call +91-1800-123-4567 (Mon-Sat, 9 AM - 7 PM IST).

Q: Is Cash on Delivery available for all products?
A: Cash on Delivery is available for orders below Rs. 20,000. Orders above this amount require prepaid payment.

Q: Can I get an invoice/GST bill for my purchase?
A: Yes, a GST-compliant invoice is automatically generated and available for download from the "My Orders" section, or emailed after delivery.
