REFUND & RETURN POLICY - Customer Support

Return Window:
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Certain categories (listed below) have different windows.

Category-Specific Return Windows:
- Smartphones, Laptops, Tablets: 10 days from delivery
- Headphones, Earbuds, Speakers: 7 days from delivery (must be unopened/unused due to hygiene reasons)
- Smartwatches, Accessories, Cables: 30 days from delivery
- Smart Home Devices: 15 days from delivery

Conditions for Return:
- Item must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging
- All accessories, manuals, and freebies included with the original order must be returned
- Products marked "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase (e.g., certain clearance items) cannot be returned unless defective

Refund Process:
1. Customer initiates a return request via the "My Orders" section or by contacting support
2. Customer Support schedules a pickup (where available) or asks the customer to ship the item back
3. Once the returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse, the refund is approved or rejected within 2 business days
4. Approved refunds are credited within 5-7 business days to the original payment method
5. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer or UPI after the customer provides account details

Exchanges:
Customers may request a size, color, or model exchange within the applicable return window, subject to stock availability. Exchanges follow the same pickup and inspection process as returns.

Damaged or Defective Items:
If a product arrives damaged or defective, customers should report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos/videos. In such cases, Customer Support offers a free replacement or full refund, and return shipping is free of charge.

Cancellations:
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once shipped, the order must be returned using the standard return process after delivery.
