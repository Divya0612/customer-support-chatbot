SHIPPING POLICY - Customer Support

Delivery Timelines:
- Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune): 2-3 business days
- Tier 2 cities: 3-5 business days
- Remote or rural areas: 5-8 business days
- Express Delivery (select pin codes only): Next-day delivery for orders placed before 12 PM

Shipping Charges:
- Free standard shipping on all orders above Rs. 999
- Orders below Rs. 999: flat shipping fee of Rs. 79
- Express Delivery: additional Rs. 149 charge, where available

Order Tracking:
Once an order is shipped, customers receive a tracking link via email and SMS. Orders can also be tracked by logging into the Customer Support account under "My Orders."

International Shipping:
Currently, Customer Support ships only within India. International shipping is not supported at this time.

Delayed or Lost Shipments:
If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window by more than 2 days, customers should contact support for an update. If a package is confirmed lost in transit, Customer Support will offer a full refund or a free replacement, whichever the customer prefers.

Packaging:
All items are shipped in tamper-proof, secure packaging. Customers are encouraged to record an unboxing video for high-value items (above Rs. 10,000) to assist with claims in case of damage or missing parts.

Multiple Item Orders:
If an order contains multiple items, they may be shipped separately from different warehouses and can arrive on different dates.
